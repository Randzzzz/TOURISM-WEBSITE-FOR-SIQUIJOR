Activities

From pristine beaches to mystical sites and local delicacies, Siquijor offers endless adventures for every traveler to enjoy.

Beach Hopping

Relax on the pristine shores of Salagdoong, Paliton, and Kagusuan Beach.

Waterfall Trekking

Take a refreshing dip in Cambugahay Falls, Lugnason Falls, and Kawasan Falls.

Island Sightseeing

Visit historic sites like St. Francis of Assisi Church and the centuries-old Balete Tree.

Cave Exploring

Venture into Cantabon Cave and experience its underground wonders.

Food Tasting

Savor local delicacies like torta, pan bisaya, and fresh seafood.

Sunset Watching

Witness breathtaking sunsets at Paliton Beach or Larena Triad View Deck.

Diving & Snorkeling

Explore vibrant coral reefs and marine life in Tulapos Marine Sanctuary.

Motorbike Touring

Rent a motorbike and ride through Siquijor’s scenic coastal and mountain roads.

Frequently Asked Questions

Siquijor Travel Guide: FAQs for a Hassle-Free Adventure

How do I get to Siquijor?

You can reach Siquijor by ferry from Dumaguete, Cebu, Bohol, or Iligan, with regular trips available from major ports. The nearest airport is in Dumaguete, just a short ferry ride away.

Is Siquijor safe for tourists?

Yes! Siquijor is known for being a peaceful and welcoming island, with friendly locals and a low crime rate.

What is the best time to visit Siquijor?

The best time to visit is during the dry season (November to May) when the weather is ideal for outdoor activities and sightseeing.
