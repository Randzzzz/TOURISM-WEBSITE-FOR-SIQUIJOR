FIG Sunset View Resort

Nestled along the pristine shores of Siquijor, FIG Sunset View Resort offers a peaceful retreat where guests can unwind in the beauty of nature. With breathtaking ocean views and stunning sunsets, this resort provides a serene atmosphere perfect for those seeking relaxation and tranquility.

Whether you're looking to lounge by the shore, explore the island’s hidden gems, or indulge in local flavors, this beachfront paradise caters to every type of traveler. Experience warm hospitality, modern comforts, and the soothing sounds of the sea at FIG Sunset View Resort.
