Dolce Amore Resort

Bringing a touch of European charm to Siquijor, Dolce Amore Resort offers an intimate and romantic retreat for travelers looking for comfort and elegance. Inspired by the warmth of Italian hospitality, this resort is perfect for couples, families, and friends seeking a serene escape by the sea.

With stylish accommodations, a cozy ambiance, and a restaurant serving authentic Italian and Filipino cuisine, Dolce Amore Resort is where island charm meets European sophistication. Enjoy peaceful mornings with ocean views and relaxing evenings under the stars at this hidden gem.
